### 🔍 Key Business Insights

1. **South Overloaded:** 57M pop (39%) but 4.8Bn records (37%) + 11.8M reports — 2.1x Northeast. 82 agencies vs 33 NE.
2. **Larceny Dominates:** 10M Larceny-Theft = 62% of all 8 offense types — single focus area.
3. **Clearance Crisis:** 6M / 29M = 20.7% clearance rate. South 2.5M clears 2x Midwest 1.3M, Northeast 1.1M lowest.
4. **Florida Data Gap:** Florida County #10 4.8M + Municipal #13 3.4M = 63% of worst performers — likely reporting failure.
5. **Municipal Backbone:** 88K offenses (55%), serves 79M (54%), clears 3.3M (55%) — does majority of FBI work.
6. **2022 Spike:** 4.1M in 2022 peak vs 1.2M in 2016 — 3.4x increase, post-COVID or NIBRS migration.

### ✅ Recommendations

1. **Balance South -> Northeast:** Move resources from South (82 agencies overloaded) to Northeast (33 agencies under-utilized).
2. **Fix Larceny-Theft:** 10M cases = 62% — create dedicated task force, focus on Texas (2.7M) + California.
3. **Audit Florida:** Investigate Florida #10 (4.8M) & #13 (3.4M) — 8.2M records gap, data quality issue.
4. **Boost Clearance:** NE/West 1.1-1.2M vs South 2.5M — benchmark South clearance process to NE/West.
5. **Resource Municipal Police:** 79M pop, 88K offenses, 3.3M cleared — needs staffing, it's 55% of system.
6. **Add Staff CA/TX:** CA 13.5M + TX 12.9M top served, plus TX State Police #5 only 3.1M served but high reports — understaffed.
